How it works

Four short steps. Then it runs on its own.

01

Say what goes quiet.

Pick the apps from Apple's own picker, whole categories if you like, and the websites you keep opening in Safari. One switch blocks every adult site.

  • Apps
  • Categories
  • Websites
  • Adult sites
02

Choose what a pause costs.

A pause opens the apps for a while. It can cost nothing, ten seconds, a typed passage, three sums, a steady hand, your code, a partner's code, or a charger. And you set how many pauses a day. Zero is allowed.

  • Nine costs
  • 0 to 5 pauses a day
03

Tie it to a place.

Hours are only half of it. A moment can run only at home, or everywhere but the office. Kept follows where you are, not only the clock, and the comparison never leaves the phone.

  • Only here
  • Everywhere but here
  • 100 m, 200 m, 500 m
04

Strict means strict.

Commit until a date and the moment cannot be softened, the pauses drop to zero, and Kept itself cannot be deleted from the phone. One emergency pass a month remains, for real emergencies.

  • Gentle
  • Firm
  • Committed
05

Progress in moments kept.

Reports count what you kept, exactly, and show how much of your evening came back from Screen Time's own figures. Never a guilt trip in minutes.

  • Week
  • Month
  • Trend

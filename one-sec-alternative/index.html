Where Kept is different

Closed hours, not a speed bump

one sec slows you down every time you open an app. Kept draws a line around part of your day. During a moment the apps stay closed, and outside it Kept leaves you alone. If a pause before every open is too easy to tap through for you, a closed window may suit you better.

Place is built in

In Kept, every moment can depend on where you are. Block social apps only at the office, or block work mail everywhere but the office. You pick the place on a map, with a radius of 100, 200 or 500 metres, and the rule works with the app closed. In one sec, location needs an iOS Focus that turns on at a place.

A pause has a price, and a limit

Each moment has its own price for a pause: nothing, a 10-second wait, a typed passage, three sums, catching a moving dot, turning the phone, your own code, a partner's code, or being on a charger. You also decide how many pauses a day, from zero to five. Zero means the apps stay closed until the moment ends.

Committed means until a date

Committed locks a moment until a date you choose. Until then it cannot be softened, the pauses drop to zero, and Kept cannot be deleted from the phone. One emergency pass a month remains, for real emergencies.

When one sec is the better choice

  • You want a gentle pause, all day. one sec's pause is its core, and it is well studied. A 2023 study in PNAS found that one sec cut the opening of target apps by 57% after six weeks.
  • You use more than an iPhone. one sec runs on iPad, Mac and Android, and in desktop browsers. Kept is for iPhone only.
  • You want a free version or a lower yearly price. one sec has a free version for one app, and one sec pro costs $19.99 a year in the US. There is also a lifetime purchase.
  • You want reflection tools. one sec has journaling, intention tracking and healthy alternatives.
  • You want a long track record. one sec has about 24,000 ratings at 4.8 stars in the US App Store.

Questions

Does Kept show a pause every time I open an app, like one sec?

No. Outside its moments, Kept leaves your apps alone. During a moment the apps are closed, and a pause is the only way back in. Each pause has the price you set, and you choose how many pauses a day, from zero to five.

Can Kept block apps by location?

Yes. Any moment can run only at a place, or everywhere but a place, with a radius of 100, 200 or 500 metres. It works with the app closed.

Is Kept cheaper than one sec?

Not by the year. In the US App Store, one sec pro costs $19.99 a year and Kept costs $29.99 a year after a seven-day trial. By the month, Kept costs $4.99 and one sec pro costs $6.99.

Does Kept work on iPad, Mac or Android?

No. Kept is for iPhone only, with iOS 17 or later. one sec also has iPad, Mac and Android apps, and browser extensions.

Can I use Kept and one sec at the same time?

Yes. You can keep one sec's pause for the whole day and add Kept for the hours and places where an app must stay closed.

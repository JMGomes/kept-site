Where Kept is different

Place is built in

In Kept, every moment can depend on where you are. Block social apps only at the office, or block work mail everywhere but the office. You pick the place on a map, with a radius of 100, 200 or 500 metres. Kept uses the region monitoring in iOS, so the rule works with the app closed, and the comparison stays on the phone. In Opal, location needs an Apple Shortcuts automation that starts a timer.

You set the price of a pause

A pause opens the apps again for a short time. In Kept, each moment has its own price for a pause: nothing, a 10-second wait, a typed passage, three sums, catching a moving dot, turning the phone, your own code, a partner's code, or being on a charger. You also decide how many pauses a day, from zero to five.

Committed means until a date

Firm locks a moment while it runs. Committed locks it until a date you choose. Until then the moment cannot be softened, the pauses drop to zero, and Kept cannot be deleted from the phone. One emergency pass a month remains, for real emergencies.

No account, and a lower price

Kept has no account and no server. Your moments and places stay on your phone and in your own iCloud. After the seven-day trial, Kept costs $29.99 a year, against $99.99 a year for Opal Pro.

When Opal is the better choice

  • You use a Mac or an Android phone. Opal has apps for both. Kept is for iPhone only.
  • You want daily time limits or open limits. Opal can block an app after a daily allowance or a number of opens. Kept blocks by hours and place.
  • You want a free plan. Opal has a free tier. Kept has a seven-day trial.
  • You like scores and company. Opal has the Opal Score, streaks, friends and a leaderboard.
  • You want a long track record. Opal has about 90,000 ratings at 4.7 stars in the US App Store.

Questions

Is Kept free, like Opal's free tier?

No. Kept has a seven-day free trial, then costs $29.99 a year or $4.99 a month in the US. There is no free tier.

Can Kept block apps by location?

Yes. Any moment can run only at a place, or everywhere but a place, with a radius of 100, 200 or 500 metres. It works with the app closed.

Does Kept have a hard mode like Opal?

Kept has two. Firm locks a moment while it runs. Committed locks it until a date you choose: no pauses, no softer settings, and Kept cannot be deleted from the phone. One emergency pass a month remains.

Does Kept work on Mac or Android?

No. Kept is for iPhone only, with iOS 17 or later. Opal also has Mac and Android apps.

Can I use Kept and Opal at the same time?

Yes. Both use Apple's Screen Time, and an app that either one blocks stays blocked. You can try Kept before you change anything in Opal.
